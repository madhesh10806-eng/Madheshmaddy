What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that enables machines to learn, think, solve problems, recognize patterns, understand language, and make decisions similar to humans.

Learning

AI learns from large amounts of data using algorithms instead of being programmed for every task.

Prediction

AI predicts future outcomes by analyzing previous information and identifying hidden patterns.

Understanding

AI understands text, voice, and images using Natural Language Processing and Computer Vision.

Automation

AI automates repetitive tasks, improves productivity, and assists people in many industries.
